## 5. Hard-Banned Words and Phrases

These words never appear in copy. The list is hard. There are no contextual exceptions.

> Cheap, Cheapest, Low-cost, Limited time offer, Act now, Hurry up, Buy now, Don't miss out, Instant results, Expert, Could improve, Hopefully, Weak students, Poor performers

Additional bans:

- Any vague superlative not backed by a number from Section 1 — "the best," "world-class," "unmatched," "leading."
- Passive uncommitted language — "might," "could possibly," "we try to."
- Sales-script language — "Buy Now," "Act Now," "Sign Up Immediately," "Don't Miss Out."
- Generic openers — "In today's digital world," "Education is important," "It is no secret that…"
- "Free" used to imply pricing tier — "free trial," "free first month," "free service." The only approved use of "free" is "Book a Free Demo" or "Free academic counselling" (because both are factually free).

### Regulatory and Location Names

City names (Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Doha, Dammam, Muscat, Kuwait City, Beirut, etc.) are **allowed** when the page is about that city. They are normal subject matter for location pages.

Regulatory bodies (KHDA, ADEK, SPEA, MOE) may be referenced factually — e.g. "UAE-licensed" — but never claim Tuitional is endorsed, approved, or accredited by them unless that has been formally confirmed.

## 7. Ideal Customer Profiles

### Primary ICP 1 — The Concerned Parent
- Parent of an IGCSE / GCSE / A Level student, typically expat or Gulf-national
- Locations: UAE, KSA, Qatar, Oman (primary); UK, Spain (secondary)
- Pain points: child struggling in a specific subject; exam pressure; lack of visibility into progress; previous poor experience with a tutor
- Decision triggers: upcoming exams, declining mock grades, peer recommendation
- Tone for this audience: reassuring, credibility-forward, outcome-specific

### Primary ICP 2 — The Student (IGCSE / A Level)
- Age 14–18, preparing for international exams
- Location: Gulf region primarily
- Pain points: struggling with specific topics, not connecting with school teaching, low confidence before exams
- Decision triggers: upcoming mocks, specific subject failure, peer recommendation
- Tone for this audience: direct, empathetic, achievement-oriented

### Secondary ICP — International / Expat Families
- Following Cambridge, IB, or AP programmes
- Value English-medium instruction and global standards
- Appreciate Tuitional's global tutor network alongside Gulf-rooted context
