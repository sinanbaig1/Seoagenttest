## E-E-A-T Signals for Copy

These signals distinguish Tuitional from generic tutoring aggregators and support Google's quality assessment of the pages.

### Experience Signals
- Tutors have marked real exam board papers (Cambridge CAIE, Edexcel, AQA)
- Sessions use past papers from the current exam series — not out-of-date questions
- Tuitional has delivered 120,000+ tutoring hours — real-world accumulated experience
- Students assigned a dedicated Academic Consultant from first contact

### Expertise Signals
- Every tutor holds a relevant degree in their subject area
- 16-step vetting process — not a marketplace where anyone can sign up
- Tutors trained on the specific specification code for each board
- Tutors know mark scheme requirements — not just subject content
- Board-specific training: Core vs Extended (CAIE), Foundation vs Higher (Edexcel/AQA), HL vs SL (IB)

### Authoritativeness Signals
- 4.7 on Trustpilot — cite this by name
- UAE-licensed and registered
- Covers all major boards under one platform (CAIE, Edexcel, AQA, IB, AP, OCR)
- Serves students in 10+ countries

### Trustworthiness Signals
- Matching speed: under 10 minutes — a specific, verifiable claim (canonical phrasing in `facts.md` §1)
- Parent dashboard gives full visibility: attendance, schedule, tutor communication
- Free academic counselling — included, not paywalled
- Mock exams and progress reports — included in the service
