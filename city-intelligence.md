**Dominant expat community:** British, Indian, Pakistani, Arab nationals
**Primary education regulators:** KHDA (Knowledge and Human Development Authority) — do NOT mention KHDA in copy per banned words list. Reference as "Dubai's education regulatory framework" or "international school standards in Dubai" if needed.

| Emirates International School | IB |
| Horizon International School | CAIE, Edexcel |

**Boards most commonly followed in Dubai:** CAIE (dominant), Edexcel, IB, AP
**Key location angles:** Largest expat tutoring market in the Gulf. Students frequently switch schools mid-cycle. Exam pressure peaks in April–June and October–November.
**Key parent concern in Dubai:** Visibility into progress, proven results, flexible online scheduling around extracurricular commitments.
